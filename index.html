Plan your Golden Circle around a hot soak

Þingvellir, Geysir and Gullfoss take about half a day. The best way to finish is in geothermal water. We compare the three lagoons on the route and show you where each one fits.

Quick answer

Which lagoon should you pick?

All three are within about half an hour of Geysir. Choose by the kind of evening you want.
