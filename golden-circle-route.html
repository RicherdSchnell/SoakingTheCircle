Self-drive itinerary

A one-day Golden Circle with a lagoon finish

The classic loop takes most of a day by car. This plan saves the best for last: a late-afternoon soak before the drive back to Reykjavík.

Laugarás Lagoon or Secret LagoonBook a late-afternoon slot, then drive back about 1.5 hours.

Prefer a mid-day soak? Swap the order: stop at Laugarvatn Fontana between Þingvellir and Geysir, then finish at Gullfoss. Compare the lagoons.

Seasons

Summer vs winter on the Golden Circle

SUMMER

Long days, busy sites

Near-endless daylight means no rush. Arrive at Geysir and Gullfoss early or late to avoid tour bus peaks.

WINTER

Short days, dark soaks

Only a few hours of daylight in December. Leave at first light, check road conditions, and book the lagoon for after dark.

Practical

Driving tips

  • The Golden Circle roads are paved, so a 2WD car is fine in summer. In winter, choose a car with good winter tyres and check road conditions before you leave.
  • Check the weather at vedur.is. Wind is often the bigger problem than snow.
  • Parking is paid at Þingvellir. Pay at the machine or online.
  • Many Icelandic fuel pumps are self-service and need a card with a PIN, so know yours before you set off.
